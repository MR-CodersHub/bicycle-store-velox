Our Origin

It started with a slipping chain and a skipped race.

In 2018, our founder missed the qualifying cutoff for an endurance brevet due to a sheared derailleur hanger and an improperly tensioned chain that a local shop refused to warrant. He realized that bicycle retail was broken: bikes were sold in cardboard boxes with zero fitting, and aftercare was nonexistent.

Velox Cycles opened on Khader Nawaz Khan Road with one foundational rule: we will never roll a bicycle out of our doors that our master technicians haven't personally built, torqued to manufacturer spec, and laser-fitted to the rider.

Eight years later, our workshop is an official Shimano Service Center (SSC). We host Chennai's largest free weekend group ride with over 1,400 peloton members, and we have tuned over 15,000 bicycles across Tamil Nadu and South India.
