Bicycle Sizing & 3D Fitting

Bicycle sizing is based on height and inseam standover: Small (50-52cm for 160-170cm), Medium (54cm for 170-178cm), Large (56cm for 178-185cm), and Extra Large (58cm+ for 185cm+). Our studio offers complimentary 3D laser dynamic fitting with all purchases.
